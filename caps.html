Behind the thinking

When creating this project, my personal prompt to myself was: ‘I should put my thinking cap on, but what would it look like…?’

That question became the starting point for a short poem, which then inspired the design and the interactive experience on this webpage.

The video was scripted from my freewriting, then filmed and edited over two days. The website itself came together in just one.

It all culminated in this little experience, and I hope you enjoyed it as much as I enjoyed bringing it to life.
